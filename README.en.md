**Automated perimeter system for proactive defense against forest and urban fires.**

## What problem does it solve?

Current fire response is reactive: firefighters, planes, helicopters. By the time they arrive, the fire is already advanced. Communities, industries, and valuable forests are left exposed.

**WPD changes the paradigm. It is a proactive defense.**

## What does it do?

A network of fixed perimeter stations that detect fire before it reaches the critical zone and automatically activate a water barrier.

**Components of each station:**
- **Concrete enclosure** with water reservoir (buried or semi-buried).
- **Vertical pole** (metal or concrete) that raises the sprinkler.
- **Sprinkler** at the top of the pole.
- **Sensors** (heat, smoke, gases) integrated.
- **AI** for detection and automatic activation.

## Why is it different?

- **Proactive**: acts before the fire arrives, not after.
- **Automated**: no human intervention needed.
- **Fixed infrastructure**: once installed, it always protects the same area.
- **Scalable**: hundreds of stations can be installed along a perimeter.

## Who is it for?

- Rural communities in fire-risk areas.
- Forestry and agricultural industries.
- National parks and nature reserves.
- Wildland-urban interface (communities next to forests).
